# Damodaran Lens

## Source Inputs

Compact execution card with optional source cross-check:

- `references/masters/source_materials/ai_hedge_fund/src/agents/aswath_damodaran.py`

## Core Use

Use Damodaran to connect narrative, lifecycle stage, risk, reinvestment, and valuation model choice.

## Best For

- Cross-company model selection
- Companies changing lifecycle stage
- Growth-to-maturity transitions
- Complex narratives that need numerical discipline
- SOTP and story-to-number checks

## Key Questions

- What is the company story?
- Which numbers must be true for that story to work?
- Where is the company in its lifecycle?
- What risks belong in cash flows versus discount rate?
- Is the valuation model matched to the business economics?

## Required Evidence

- Lifecycle classification
- Revenue growth and margin path
- Reinvestment rate
- Cost of capital
- Segment economics
- Narrative risks and numerical constraints

## Valuation Preference

- Lifecycle-adjusted DCF
- SOTP
- Scenario valuation
- Cost-of-capital and reinvestment sensitivity

## Downgrade The Lens When

- The narrative cannot be translated into measurable assumptions.
- Data quality is too weak for model selection.
- A specialist regulatory model should dominate, such as bank, insurer, or REIT analysis.
