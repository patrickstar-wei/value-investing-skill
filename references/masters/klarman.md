# Klarman Lens

## Core Use

Use Klarman when uncertainty is high and the most important task is avoiding permanent capital loss while preserving upside asymmetry.

## Best For

- Special situations
- Distressed or stressed assets
- Complex balance sheets
- Spinoffs, restructurings, liquidations, or litigation-driven situations
- High-uncertainty valuation

## Key Questions

- What can go wrong permanently?
- Is the downside protected by assets, cash flow, structure, or price?
- Is the payoff asymmetric?
- What catalyst or path unlocks value?
- Is patience compensated?

## Required Evidence

- Downside asset or cash-flow support
- Debt maturity and covenant profile
- Legal, regulatory, or restructuring facts
- Catalyst map
- Bear-case liquidation or recovery value

## Valuation Preference

- Liquidation value
- NAV / recovery value
- Scenario-weighted valuation
- Conservative SOTP

## Downgrade The Lens When

- There is no clear downside anchor.
- The thesis depends only on multiple expansion.
- The catalyst is vague or not economically material.
