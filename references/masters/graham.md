# Graham Lens

## Source Inputs

Compact execution card with optional source cross-check:

- `references/masters/source_materials/ai_hedge_fund/src/agents/ben_graham.py`

## Core Use

Use Graham when downside protection, balance-sheet strength, asset backing, or low-confidence data matters more than optimistic growth.

## Best For

- Deep value
- Distressed or neglected companies
- Asset-heavy businesses
- Cyclicals near trough valuation
- Situations with uncertain forecasts

## Key Questions

- What is the conservative value if growth is zero?
- Is there real asset protection after liabilities?
- Does the price offer a margin of safety against conservative value?
- Are earnings normalized or temporarily inflated?
- Could permanent capital loss occur even if the stock looks cheap?

## Required Evidence

- Balance sheet quality
- Net debt and liquidity
- Normalized earnings or cash flow
- Asset value, liquidation value, or EPV support
- Downside scenario

## Valuation Preference

- EPV / no-growth value
- NAV / liquidation value
- Mid-cycle earnings
- Conservative DCF only when cash flows are stable

## Downgrade The Lens When

- Intangible franchise value dominates and asset value is not meaningful.
- The business requires large reinvestment just to stand still.
- Accounting assets are stale, impaired, or hard to monetize.
