# Howard Marks Lens

## Core Use

Use Howard Marks when cycles, risk appetite, market expectations, or second-level thinking are central to the investment outcome.

## Best For

- Cyclicals
- Semiconductors
- Commodities
- Credit-sensitive businesses
- Expensive markets with optimistic expectations

## Key Questions

- Where are we in the cycle?
- What does the market already believe?
- Is risk being priced correctly?
- Are current margins, demand, or multiples above mid-cycle levels?
- What is the second-level view that differs from consensus?

## Required Evidence

- Cycle indicators
- Supply / demand balance
- Mid-cycle margins or volumes
- Inventory and pricing data
- Credit and interest-rate sensitivity
- Reverse DCF or implied expectations

## Valuation Preference

- Mid-cycle valuation
- Scenario-weighted valuation
- Reverse DCF
- Stress-case FCF

## Downgrade The Lens When

- The company has stable, non-cyclical cash flows.
- Cycle data is missing or stale.
- The conclusion cannot distinguish normalized economics from peak conditions.
