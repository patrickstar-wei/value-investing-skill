# Mauboussin / Rappaport Lens

## Core Use

Use Mauboussin / Rappaport when the core question is what expectations are embedded in the current stock price.

## Best For

- High-expectation stocks
- Reverse DCF
- Growth stocks where narrative dominates
- Market-implied growth and margin analysis
- Consensus-versus-own-assumption comparison

## Key Questions

- What revenue growth, margin, and reinvestment assumptions does the current price imply?
- Are those assumptions plausible versus history, peers, and industry capacity?
- What must go right for the market price to be justified?
- Where is the most important sensitivity?
- Is there a variant perception?

## Required Evidence

- Current price and share count
- Net debt / cash
- Revenue base and margins
- Reinvestment needs
- Discount rate and terminal assumptions
- Peer or industry benchmarks

## Valuation Preference

- Reverse DCF
- Expectations bridge
- Scenario DCF
- Sensitivity table

## Downgrade The Lens When

- Current price or capital structure data is stale.
- Forecast drivers cannot be reasonably bounded.
- The company is better valued by asset value, regulated capital, or liquidation value.
