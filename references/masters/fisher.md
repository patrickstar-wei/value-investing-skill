# Fisher Lens

## Source Inputs

Compact execution card with optional source cross-check:

- `references/masters/source_materials/ai_hedge_fund/src/agents/phil_fisher.py`

## Core Use

Use Fisher when long-term growth quality, management quality, product depth, and reinvestment runway drive most of the value.

## Best For

- High-quality growth companies
- Technology-enabled businesses
- Category leaders with expanding markets
- Companies with visible innovation cycles

## Key Questions

- Is growth durable or only cyclical?
- Does the company have a long reinvestment runway?
- Is management strengthening the competitive position?
- Are R&D, product quality, and customer value visible in results?
- Can margins improve without damaging growth?

## Required Evidence

- Revenue growth durability
- Gross margin and operating leverage
- Customer retention or demand quality
- R&D productivity
- Segment growth and profitability
- Management execution record

## Valuation Preference

- Scenario DCF
- TAM penetration model
- Unit economics model
- SOTP for separable growth engines

## Downgrade The Lens When

- Growth is mostly acquisition-driven.
- Product advantage cannot be connected to financial results.
- Market size claims are unsupported.
