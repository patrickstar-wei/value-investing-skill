# Multi-master Value Investing Framework v17.2

This file is now a compact index. The full operating policy lives in `references/core/investment_philosophy_layer.md`.

| Master | Core Lens | Best Use | Default Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Graham | Margin of safety, asset protection, downside first | Deep value, distressed, asset-heavy, low-confidence inputs | Prevents overpaying and blocks weak downside protection |
| Buffett / Munger | Moat, owner earnings, compounding, capital allocation | Quality compounders, platform businesses, capital allocators | Tests business quality and long-term compounding durability |
| Fisher | Growth quality, management quality, reinvestment runway | High-quality growth and technology-enabled businesses | Tests whether growth is durable rather than merely fast |
| Klarman | Risk-first, asymmetric payoff, permanent loss avoidance | Special situations, distressed, high uncertainty | Forces asymmetry and downside analysis |
| Greenblatt | High return on capital + cheapness | Screens, quality-at-reasonable-price, special situations | Helps rank watchlists and peer sets |
| Howard Marks | Cycle and risk pricing | Cyclicals, semiconductors, credit-sensitive businesses | Prevents peak-cycle extrapolation |
| Greenwald | EPV and competitive advantage | Mature stable businesses | Separates no-growth value from franchise value |
| Mauboussin / Rappaport | Expectations investing | Reverse DCF, high-expectation stocks | Identifies what the market price already assumes |
| Damodaran | Narrative and numbers, lifecycle valuation | Cross-company model selection | Enforces story-to-number consistency |

## Core rule

Every workflow must pass through the Core Investment Philosophy Layer and Investment Quality Gate before producing a final action judgment.
