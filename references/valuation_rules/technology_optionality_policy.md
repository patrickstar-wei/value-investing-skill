# Technology Optionality Policy v16

## Purpose

Many mature industrial or consumer companies use technology language: AI, automation, robotics, smart manufacturing, energy technology, healthcare technology, industrial software, or digital platforms.

The skill must not automatically value these companies as pure technology stocks. Technology exposure is an **Overlay**, not an automatic Base Type change.

## Technology Categories

### 1. Efficiency Enabler

Technology improves the core business but does not create a separable business segment.

Examples:

- Automation and smart factories
- AI quality control
- Digital supply-chain management
- Smart inventory planning
- Industrial internet used internally

Valuation treatment:

- No separate SOTP value
- Reflect through margin durability, cost advantage, inventory turns, FCF conversion, ROIC, and moat quality

### 2. Separable Monetized Segment

Technology is a business line with visible revenue, customers, growth, and a credible profit path.

Examples:

- Robotics and automation equipment
- Industrial software
- Smart building technology
- Energy management systems
- Medical technology devices

Valuation treatment:

- Use SOTP if segment data is material and separable
- Use comparable multiples only as cross-check
- Use scenario-weighted optionality when contribution is emerging but uncertain

### 3. Narrative-only Optionality

Technology is mainly a label or strategy, without visible revenue, customers, profits, or segment data.

Valuation treatment:

- No separate valuation premium
- Mention as low-confidence upside optionality only

## Evidence Required for Technology Premium

A technology premium requires evidence in at least several of the following:

- Separate segment disclosure
- Revenue contribution
- Growth above the core business
- Gross margin / operating margin or credible path to margin
- Commercial customers / orders / backlog
- Capital efficiency or improving ROIC
- TAM expansion that is economically monetizable
- Strategic fit with the core business

## User-facing output rule

Default reports should show:

- Technology Optionality: Monetized / Emerging / Efficiency-only / Narrative-only
- Valuation treatment: SOTP / scenario optionality / reflected in core model / no separate premium
- Evidence quality: High / Medium / Low
- Investment implication

Do not show detailed optionality calculations unless explicitly requested.
